What this app is

A personal record-keeping and appointment-preparation tool with safety education. What it is intended to do: store what you choose to record, display your own history, prepare printed summaries for appointments, and link to official sources (FDA, DailyMed) so you can verify information yourself. What it does not do: it does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition; it does not make or suggest medical decisions; it never recommends, normalizes, or ranks doses; and nothing in it is medical advice.

Not medical advice; no dosing

  • Profiles are not passwords — anyone who can use this device can open every profile.
  • Do not store other people's health information in this app without their consent.
