What "regulatory status" means — Learn more
  • "Approved products exist" is an ingredient-level statement: FDA-approved prescription products exist for this ingredient, but approval applies only to those specific products. Other versions — compounded and "research" versions of the same molecule — are not approved.
  • "No FDA-approved product" means no FDA-approved human use exists for that ingredient at all.
  • Approval is verified per exact product on DailyMed — never per ingredient.
  • "Source verified" means the app's developer checked the cited official page on the review date — not qualified clinical review, never implying safety or endorsement.
If you feel unwell — Learn more

Severe symptoms — trouble breathing, swelling of face/throat, chest pain, fainting, rapidly worsening reactions — need emergency medical care immediately. Show clinicians exactly what you took (your export file is designed for that). For anything milder but persistent, log it and raise it with a licensed healthcare provider.

Where your data lives

Learn more

This app is fully offline: your records are stored in this browser's IndexedDB on this device, and no server receives them. Clearing the browser's site data (or uninstalling the installed app) deletes every record permanently. The only network requests are loading the app shell and the official-source links you choose to open — no personal data is included in those links.
